Summarizer & Voice Nav

Click "Summarize this page" or say "summarize" after starting voice control.
Voice commands
  • "summarize" — summarize the current page
  • "read" / "read summary" — read the summary aloud
  • "stop" — stop reading
  • "scroll down" / "scroll up" — scroll the page
  • "go to top" / "go to bottom" — jump to top/bottom
  • "go back" / "go forward" — browser history navigation
  • "switch to [language]" — change summary language, e.g. "switch to French"
